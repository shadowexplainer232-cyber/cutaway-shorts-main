CUTAWAY - EPISODE 001 - NARRATION RECORDING SHEET

Make ONE audio clip per scene (mp3, wav or m4a). Name each clip with its number:
01.mp3, 02.mp3 ... 07.mp3. Speak clearly, confident and natural, a little energetic.
Leave about half a second of silence at the start and end of each clip. Keep each clip to the length of its text (about 3-9 seconds).
Free text-to-speech websites or your phone's voice recorder both work. Check the tool's licence allows YouTube use.

--- 01.mp3  (hook) ---
Three out of every four strokes in your engine make no power at all. So how does it keep running?

--- 02.mp3  (intake) ---
First, intake. The piston moves down and the intake valve opens, pulling air and fuel into the cylinder.

--- 03.mp3  (compression) ---
Second, compression. Both valves close, and the piston rises, squeezing the mixture.

--- 04.mp3  (power) ---
Third, power. The spark plug ignites the mixture, and the expanding gas shoves the piston down.

--- 05.mp3  (exhaust) ---
Fourth, exhaust. The exhaust valve opens, and the rising piston pushes the burnt gas out.

--- 06.mp3  (answer) ---
That is two full turns of the crankshaft per power stroke. The flywheel and the other cylinders carry the engine through the rest.

--- 07.mp3  (outro) ---
This is Cutaway. Understand how the world works. Follow for the next machine.
